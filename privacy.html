Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026. Политика конфиденциальности (русская версия ниже).

What we collect

  • Google account basics (scopes openid, email, profile): your name, email address and profile picture — used to create and show your Lazzy profile.
  • Google Drive: drive.appdata — a hidden app folder on your own Drive that stores your liked tracks and playlists for sync; drive.file — only files you choose to upload through the app (your own tracks), shared by link so other listeners can play them. The app cannot see any other files on your Drive.
  • Profile data you enter: display name, @handle, bio, avatar, banner, followers/following, likes, playlists and their visibility (public / friends / private). Stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and shown to other users according to your visibility settings.
  • Email sign-in (optional): your email address and a password handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see the password.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • Audio files on your device are never uploaded unless you explicitly upload a track.

Processing

Profile names, bios and images may be sent to an AI moderation service (Pollinations.ai) solely to check them for prohibited content. AI features you enable (search, «Моя волна») send your text prompt to the AI provider you choose. API keys you add are stored encrypted on your device only.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Lazzy Sounds' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Sign out and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your Lazzy profile and everything stored in Firestore, email titer5249@gmail.com; it is deleted within 30 days. Files in your Drive stay under your control.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13.
